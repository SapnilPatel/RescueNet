# Hackathon Context — Dual-Use Hackathon @ USC Viterbi (Oct 16–18, 2026)

## Basics
- Hosts: **Innovate x LA** + USC Viterbi Grand Challenge Scholars, part of **LA Tech Week**.
- Kickoff: Fri Oct 16, 6–8 PM, Michelson Center (MCB) 101. Ends Sun Oct 18, 5 PM with pitch day.
- Format: one mystery problem + "twists" dropped during the event. Teams of 3+.
- Unofficial motto: "The best squads don't pick a lane, they find the solution that solves it **twice: locally and globally**."

## Mystery prompt: PREPARING FOR DISASTER
- Build a **dual-use solution to prevent shutdowns in crisis**
- **Resilient** systems in the face of disaster
- Think **efficiency, effectiveness, & cost**

## "Two Lives, One Tool" (core framing)
- **Life #1 – Ordinary day:** saves money, time or worry, so everyday users keep it and pay for it.
- **Life #2 – The crisis:** matters when things go wrong, and it is **already in place**. Users: responders, dispatchers, defenders.

**RescueNet fits Vertical 03 (Comms) + Retell**, with optional Energy (BASE batteries for beacons).

## Judging — five criteria, 1–5 each
1. Dual-use fit
2. Technical execution / MVP
3. Feasibility and impact
4. Adapting to the twists
5. Demo and pitch

## Twists (optional, but include where natural — don't force)
1. **Sat 1:43 AM – Government funding pathway:** identify ≥1 federal funding pathway (agency/program/military). *We stay civilian.*
2. **Sat 4:30 AM – Make it global:** how does the solution work in another part of the world?
3. **Sat 7:00 AM – Poster it!** Poster submitted with Devpost by **Sat 10 PM**, printed for Demo Day. 4 open awards incl. **Best Demo** (open to all, finalist or not).

## Prizes
- 1st $1,000 · 2nd $500 · 3rd $200 · special prizes for twist winners.
- **Top 3 → Das Family Incubator** (spring) to compete for a share of **$30K**. Requires ≥1 USC student on team.

## Das Family Incubator (Innovate x LA: Das Family Student Competition)
- Run by USC Civil & Environmental Engineering, funded by Santanu K. Das (CEO, EIRIS).
- Monthly workshops, mentorship, Demo Day for investors.
- Past winners: **Fire Warden (2025, $20K)** — kit turning backyard pools into wildfire defense; **MatterFlow (2026)**; FungiFix (2024).
- Judges praised "resourcefulness, accessibility, and potential to scale across sectors" + empowering vulnerable communities.

## Patterns from recent hackathon winners (researched)
Winners tend to: use voice/SMS over new apps; work offline/low-bandwidth; remove paperwork; target one specific person's pain; include equity/multilingual angle; have a visceral live demo (hardcoded fallback); use sponsor tech.
Examples: FaceTimeOS (Cal Hacks 12.0 1st), Shepherd (TreeHacks 2026 grand prize), FireForm (UCSC 1st), Firelink (UN @ UCSD 1st), Sentinel (ElevenLabs hack 1st), TRACE (AI x City Climate 1st).
